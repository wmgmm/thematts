    How this is scored
    • This is a teaching model, not a measurement. The live runs in Exercise 05 are the measurement. The rules below are fixed and simple so you can argue with them.
    • Speed starts at 100. A human costs 10 at the brief, 45 at the draft, 20 at the check and 10 at sign off. An AI costs 5 per stage. Nobody costs nothing.
    • Errors caught starts at 55 for an AI draft or 70 for a human draft, then adds 15 for a human brief (the rules are written down), 5 for an AI brief, 25 for a human check, 12 for an AI check (format and consistency, not truth) and 5 for a human sign off. If an AI drafted and an AI checked and the only human is at the end without a written brief, that final 5 is lost: the human skims because two agents already "checked".
    • Human effort counts 1 for the brief, 4 for drafting, 2 for checking, 1 for signing, out of 8. Six or more is flagged as fatigue.
    • Accountability is Low if a human signs off, Medium if a human checked or drafted but nobody signed, otherwise High.
    • Data and legal starts at the task's sensitivity, drops a level if a person reads it before it leaves and another if the brief is human-written, and is High whenever an AI drafted and no person reads it.
    • Security is Low with no AI stage. Otherwise it starts at how exposed the input is to pasted instructions, and drops a level for a human read before send and for a human brief.
    • Quality follows the errors-caught score: under 50 High, 50 to 75 Medium, above 75 Low, one level worse when the task is hard to undo.
    • People is High on the fatigue flag, Medium when a human both checks and signs off or on the rubber-stamp flag, otherwise Low.
    • Appetite: over appetite if any row is High, or if there are more Medium rows than the task allows (meeting notes 3, the student reply 1, the public page 1).
