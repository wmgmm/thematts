FOCUS ON: [OPTIONAL: WHAT TO EMPHASISE, OR LEAVE BLANK]

CONTENT
• Pick the 3–5 ideas that best explain the subject. Leave out the rest.
• Search the web if you need to check facts or add context.
• Make the most important idea the biggest and boldest.
• Lay the ideas out as a clear visual journey across one page.

DRAWING
• Explain through pictures: people, objects, simple scenes and visual metaphors.
• As few words as possible, with short, conversational headings.
• Sketchnote style: warm white paper, loose black marker outlines, natural hand lettering.
• Muted red, mustard yellow, dusty blue and soft grey.
• Arrows only where they show a connection.
• Plenty of white space. No tiny text, clutter, piles of speech bubbles or polished corporate diagrams.

Someone should get the main idea at a glance. Explain visually, don't just decorate the words.
